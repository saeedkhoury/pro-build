# Project Brief: Layla's Bakery (approved 2026-11-16)
- One-liner: a website where local customers order custom cakes for pickup.
- Goal: 30 online cake orders per month within 3 months.
- Must-have: cake gallery, custom-order form, pickup date (48h notice), contact page · Later: online deposits.
- Platform: website, mobile-first · Languages: English and Arabic (RTL) · Budget: free tiers plus a domain.
