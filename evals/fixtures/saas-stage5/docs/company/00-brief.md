# Project Brief: ChairBook (approved 2026-11-02)
- **One-liner:** online booking with no-show deposits for solo barbers and small shops.
- **Goal (3 months):** 20 paying shops.
- **Users:** customers booking; barbers managing their day · **Buyer:** shop owner ($19/chair/month).
- **Core value:** a customer books and pays a deposit in under a minute.
- **Must-have:** shop page, calendar, booking form, deposits, SMS or email reminders · **Later:** shop teams, marketplace.
- **Platform:** mobile-first web app · **Data:** customer name, phone and email; payments via Stripe.
